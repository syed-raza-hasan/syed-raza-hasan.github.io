Automating a monthly loan-level fee reconciliation

Financial controls · SQL

0.4%net variance remaining, with an audit trail on every record

Challenge

Borrowers pay for appraisals through deposits and fees, while vendors are paid separately. Finance needed to confirm each month, for every loan, that the two agreed.

Approach

Built the reconciliation in SQL, matching appraisal costs to borrower deposits and fees loan by loan, with each record traceable to its source data.

Impact

Net variance reduced to 0.4% of the total, with every remaining item traceable. The reconciliation now runs monthly without manual work.

Financial controls · SQL, data quality

Every invoicechecked against contract terms and source data each month

Challenge

Vendor invoices for loan-level services arrive monthly. Paying them unchecked risks incorrect rates, misallocated charges and duplicate payment.

Measuring marketing cost per funded loan for the CFO

Decision analysis · Marketing

~1,400channels traced from lead to funded loan

Challenge

Leadership needed to know which marketing spend was producing funded loans across HELOC, refinance and purchase products.

Approach

Built the lead-to-funded-loan funnel by channel, identified diminishing returns in the largest channel at peak spend, and used a rate-controlled correlation to test whether organic volume was hidden paid lift.

Impact

A budget reallocation recommendation to the CFO, with monthly cost guardrails and geo holdout tests before any channel receives credit.
