Automating a monthly loan-level fee reconciliation

Financial controls · SQL

0.4%net variance remaining, with an audit trail on every record

Challenge

Finance needed to confirm, every month and for every loan, that appraisal fees collected from borrowers matched what was paid to vendors.

Approach

Built the reconciliation in SQL, matching costs to deposits and fees loan by loan and keeping a traceable record for each line.

Impact

Net variance reduced to 0.4% of the total, with every remaining item traceable. The process now runs monthly without manual work.

Replacing manual invoice allocation with a pipeline

Process automation · Python, PostgreSQL

80 hoursof manual work removed each month

Challenge

Around 25 vendor invoice files a month were allocated to individual loans by hand.

Approach

Built a Python pipeline that reads each file and allocates every charge to its loan using the source loan data.

Impact

Up to 80 hours a month returned to the team, and a repeatable process in place of spreadsheets.

Measuring marketing cost per funded loan for the CFO

Decision analysis · Marketing

~1,400channels traced from lead to funded loan

Challenge

Leadership needed to know which marketing spend was producing funded loans across three loan products.

Approach

Built the lead-to-funded-loan funnel by channel and used a rate-controlled correlation to test whether organic volume was hidden paid lift.

Impact

A budget reallocation recommendation to the CFO, with monthly cost guardrails and geo holdout tests before any channel receives credit.

How engagements run

Before anything is built, four points are agreed: the question the work must answer, who will use it and how often, the decision it supports, and whether an existing report already covers it.

  1. MetricDefine the measure the decision depends on.
  2. ProblemQuantify what is wrong with it today.
  3. HypothesisState the expected cause before testing it.
  4. AnalysisTest against the data, reconciled to source.
  5. ExperimentWhere data cannot settle it, design a controlled test.
  6. DecisionAgree the action, the owner and the review date.
